Beautiful Places

Every corner of Italy has its own story.

🏛️
The Eternal City

Rome

The capital of Italy and once the heart of a huge empire. Walk past the Colosseum, toss a coin into the Trevi Fountain, and visit Vatican City, the smallest country in the world.

🛶
The Floating City

Venice

A city built on more than 100 small islands, with canals instead of streets. People get around by boat, and the famous gondolas glide under old stone bridges.

🎨
Birthplace of the Renaissance

Florence

Home of great artists like Michelangelo and Leonardo da Vinci. See the statue of David and the giant red dome of the cathedral that rises over the city.

🍋
Sea & Lemons

Amalfi Coast

Colorful villages hang on cliffs above the blue sea. The area is famous for its huge lemons and for limoncello, a sweet lemon drink.

A Short History

753 BC

Rome is founded

Legend says twin brothers Romulus and Remus started the city.

27 BC

The Roman Empire

Rome grows into an empire that rules much of Europe.

1400s

The Renaissance

A golden age of art and science begins in Florence.

1861

Italy unites

Many small states join together to become one country.

1946

A Republic

Italians vote to become the republic it is today.
